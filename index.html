Our story

Ann — because food is prasad

In Sanskrit, Ann means food: what sustains life. In Devbhoomi Haridwar we believe every plate is prasad, so our kitchen cooks only pure vegetarian food, with care for cleanliness and sattvik values.

From traditional North Indian and Garhwali dishes to live chaat counters and modern fusion, we give every event the same care, whether it's a puja for 50 guests or a bhandara for 5,000.
