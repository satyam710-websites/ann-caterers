1. Who we are

This website is run by Ann Caters & Events (“we”, “us”, “our”), a catering business based in Haridwar, Uttarakhand, India. This policy explains what personal information we handle when you visit this website or contact us through it, and how we protect it. It is written in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

2. What information we collect

This website does not collect or store any personal information by itself. There are no sign-up forms, no accounts, and no analytics or tracking tools.

We only receive information that you choose to send us:

  • WhatsApp messages: the “Get a quote” form and WhatsApp buttons do not send anything to us directly. They open WhatsApp on your device with a pre-filled message (such as occasion, number of guests, package, date and venue). Nothing is sent unless you press “Send” in WhatsApp yourself. When you do, we receive your message, your WhatsApp name and your phone number.
  • Phone calls: if you call us, we see your phone number.
  • Email: if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write.

Details you type into the quote form stay in your browser and are not saved anywhere by this website.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you send us only to:

  • reply to your enquiry and give you a quote;
  • plan, confirm and deliver catering for your event;
  • contact you about your booking (for example, menu changes, timings or payment).

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone for marketing. We will not send you promotional messages unless you ask us to.

5. Cookies

This website does not use cookies. It does not set any cookies of its own, and it does not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

6. How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and booking details (in WhatsApp, call logs or email) only as long as needed to handle your enquiry or event, and afterwards as long as needed for accounting, tax or legal reasons. After that we delete it.

7. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and how it is used;
  • ask us to correct or update inaccurate information;
  • ask us to delete your information when it is no longer needed;
  • withdraw your consent at any time;
  • raise a grievance with us and, if not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

This website is meant for adults planning events. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it was last changed.
